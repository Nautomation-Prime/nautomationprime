## 1. What the Nautomation Prime Foundation is today

The "Nautomation Prime Foundation" is a trading name and a section of this website. It is **not** a registered charity, not a registered Community Interest Company (CIC), and not a separate legal entity of any kind. It is operated directly by:

**Christopher Davies**, trading as **Nautomation Prime**
9 The Sleeve, Leek, ST13 8HR, Staffordshire, England, United Kingdom

Every commitment, free tutorial, and charity engagement made "by the Foundation" is legally a commitment made by Christopher Davies personally as a sole trader. There is no independent trustee board, no separate charitable fund, and no legal separation between "Nautomation Prime" and "Nautomation Prime Foundation" at this time.

## 2. Why we use the word "Foundation" now, and what it does not mean

"Foundation" is used here in its descriptive sense — a foundational body of free learning content and community support — and to signal genuine future intent to formalise this work as a registered charity or CIC. It is **not** used to claim, and should not be read as claiming, that:

- this initiative is a registered charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 2011;
- donations made to it receive Gift Aid or any other charitable tax treatment;
- it is regulated by the Charity Commission for England and Wales, OSCR, or the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland; or
- it is incorporated at Companies House as a CIC or any other company type.

Section 5 of the Charities Act 2011 makes it an offence to describe an institution as a registered charity when it is not one. We take that seriously, which is why this page exists and why the Foundation's own pages carry the same disclosure.

## 3. No public fundraising by the Foundation

The Foundation does not solicit donations from the public for itself, does not operate a donation button, and does not hold funds collected for charitable purposes. All free education and free/low-cost charity engineering work described on the [Foundation](../foundation/index.md) and [Charity Services](../foundation/charity-services.md) pages is paid for out of Christopher Davies' own time and the ordinary revenue of Nautomation Prime — it is not funded by public donations.

This is separate from the [Giving With Integrity](../charity.md) policy, under which Nautomation Prime intends to donate a percentage of its own *commercial* revenue to independently vetted, already-registered external charities as the business grows. If the Foundation's scope ever expands to include public fundraising, this page will be updated first, and any fundraising activity will only begin once it complies with the Charities Act 2011, the Charity Commission's fundraising rules, and, where applicable, the Fundraising Regulator's Code of Fundraising Practice.

## 4. Plans for future registration

Christopher Davies intends to formalise the Nautomation Prime Foundation as a registered charity or Community Interest Company once it reaches a scale that justifies the ongoing governance, reporting, and accounting obligations that come with either structure. Before any such registration is announced on this site, it will have already been completed and confirmed by the relevant regulator (the Charity Commission, OSCR, CCNI, or Companies House, as applicable) — this page and the Foundation pages will not describe registration as complete, in progress, or imminent until that is true.

As part of that process, and before adopting "Foundation" (or any other potentially sensitive word) in a registered company or charity name, Christopher Davies will check the name against Companies House's rules on sensitive words and expressions, and against the Charity Commission's naming guidance, and will seek professional advice on the correct structure. Nothing on this page should be relied on as confirmation that any particular name or structure has already been cleared for registration.

- Free educational content (tutorials, deep dives, guides) is provided on an "AS IS" basis with no warranty, exactly as set out in the site [Disclaimer](./disclaimer.md) and [Terms of Use](./terms-of-use.md).
- Charity engagements delivered under the [Charity Services](../foundation/charity-services.md) offer are subject to the specific liability cap and terms set out on that page, which do not exclude liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything else that cannot lawfully be excluded under the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977.
- No professional indemnity or public liability insurance level is represented on this site. Charities and individuals should ask about current insurance arrangements before relying on any free or paid engagement for critical infrastructure.

## 6. Data protection

Any information collected through a Foundation charity-services application (organisation name, registration number, contact details, and a description of the request) is processed under the same rules as every other enquiry to this site — see the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for the full detail on legal basis, retention, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 7. Equal access

Free educational content on the Foundation is available to anyone, without registration, payment, or eligibility screening of any kind. The charity-services offer applies published eligibility and capacity criteria only (see [Charity Services](../foundation/charity-services.md)) and does not discriminate on the basis of a charity's cause, beneficiaries, or protected characteristics under the Equality Act 2010.

## 8. This is not legal advice

This page is a transparency statement about how Christopher Davies has chosen to operate and describe the Nautomation Prime Foundation. It is provided for information only, is not legal advice, and should not be relied upon as such by any charity, CIC, or individual considering an engagement. If you need certainty about any of the points above for your own organisation's purposes, take independent legal advice.
