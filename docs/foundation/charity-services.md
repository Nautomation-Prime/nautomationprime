## What's on offer

Several kinds of help are available, on their own or combined, depending on what your charity actually needs — all free or low-cost:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ### Advice & improvement recommendations

    A review of your existing network — Cisco or otherwise — with plain-English, written recommendations on what to fix, replace, or improve, and why. This is assessment and advice only: nothing is changed on your infrastructure as part of it, which keeps it the lowest-risk, easiest-to-offer option and is usually free.

-   ### Cisco and general networking engineering

    Hands-on network engineer time to act on those recommendations, or on a problem you've already identified: switch, router, and wireless configuration and troubleshooting, IOS-XE upgrades, VLAN and routing changes, security hardening, and help planning or delivering a hardware refresh or office move.

-   ### Python-based network automation

    The same automation work Nautomation Prime delivers commercially, scaled to a charity's size: a CDP or access-switch audit, a configuration backup script, or a short piece of automation that solves a clearly defined, bounded problem.

-   ### Discounted larger engagements

    Where a charity's need — advice, hands-on engineering, automation, or a mix — is larger than a single free engagement can cover, work is quoted at a substantially reduced day rate against the standard [Services](../services.md) pricing, subject to capacity.

</div>

Every tutorial, deep dive, and guide in the [Learning Hub](./resources.md) is also already free to any charity, staff member, volunteer, or trustee — no application needed.

What is **not** included: ongoing managed support, 24/7 or emergency response, hardware or licence costs, or work on infrastructure that a charity's own IT provider has not agreed to. Advice and recommendations are provided in good faith based on what's found during the review, but it remains the charity's decision, and its own responsibility, whether and how to act on them. On-site visits are considered case by case and may be limited by location; remote-first delivery is the default. Nautomation Prime is a single-person practice; it cannot commit to response times or availability guarantees under this offer.

## Eligibility

To be considered, an organisation must currently meet all of the following:

1. **Be a registered UK charity or CIC**, verifiable on a public register — the [Charity Commission for England and Wales](https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/), the [Scottish Charity Register (OSCR)](https://www.oscr.org.uk/), the [Charity Commission for Northern Ireland](https://www.charitycommissionni.org.uk/), or [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/) for a CIC. Please include your registration/company number when you apply.
2. **Have a named person responsible for IT/network decisions** within the organisation who can approve changes, hold credentials, and take responsibility for maintaining backups going forward.
3. **For any engagement that changes live configuration** (as opposed to advice-only review), be able to take a current backup or export of anything that will be touched, or agree to Nautomation Prime taking one as the first step, before that work begins.
4. **Confirm any sector-specific requirements up front** — for example, if your charity works with children or vulnerable adults and requires a DBS check, safeguarding declaration, or specific vetting before anyone accesses your systems, tell us in your application so it can be arranged or, if it cannot be met, so we can say so early rather than after starting.

Applications are reviewed in the order received. Preference may be given to smaller charities, to charities local to Staffordshire and the wider Midlands, and to requests with a clearly scoped, achievable ask — but every eligible application is considered, and eligibility does not depend on a charity's cause, beneficiaries, or beliefs, other than the practical requirements above.

## How to apply

1. Email **[enquiries@nautomationprime.io](mailto:enquiries@nautomationprime.io?subject=Charity%20Support%20Application)** with the subject "Charity Support Application".
2. Include: your charity/CIC name and registration number, a link to your entry on the relevant public register, a short description of the problem you want help with (networking engineering, Python automation, or both), and the name and role of your IT/network contact.
3. Christopher will reply, on a best-efforts basis, to confirm whether the request fits current capacity and what would be involved.
4. If it goes ahead, the work is set out in a short written scope agreement (email is fine) covering what will and won't be done, timescales, and the terms below, before anything is changed on your network.
