## What to include in your message

The more specific you are, the more useful the first reply will be. Include as much of the following as you can:

- Your environment type and scale, for example branch network, campus, data centre, or mixed estate
- The operational problem you want to solve, reduce, or de-risk
- Whether this is a learning need, tooling need, or delivery need
- Any constraints such as compliance, jump hosts, change windows, or restricted workstations
- Whether you already have scripts or automation that need rescuing, replacing, or extending

---

## What happens next

1. You send an enquiry or request a discovery call.
2. Nautomation Prime replies within 24-48 hours on UK business days.
3. If a conversation makes sense, the next step is a short, no-obligation discovery discussion.
4. If the fit is right, the usual next paid step is [Pinpoint](./prime-framework/pinpoint.md), where the business case and automation priorities are clarified.

## Best-fit organisations

This contact path is best suited to teams that are:

- Running Cisco-heavy environments with repetitive, error-prone operational work
- Under pressure to prove ROI, improve governance, or reduce change risk
- Inheriting brittle scripts that nobody wants to own
- Trying to move from ad hoc automation to a sustainable operating model

If you are mainly looking for fast-turnaround, fixed-price work, start with the [SMB packages](./smb/index.md).
